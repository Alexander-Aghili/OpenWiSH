# Code of Conduct

OpenWiSH follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming to every contributor.
- Give and take criticism of the work, not of the person.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- No publishing of private information without permission.

The Covenant applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [Alexander Aghili](https://github.com/Alexander-Aghili). The maintainer reviews every report, keeps the reporter's identity private, and follows the enforcement guidelines of the Covenant: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
